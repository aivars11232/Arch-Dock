# Status of this folder: historical mapping evidence

This folder is **not** a work queue.

The three files here (`task_manifest.json`, `REQUIREMENT_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md`
and `TASK_ID_MIGRATION_FROM_86_TASK_PACK.md`) are the master plan's
requirement-mapping files, restored unchanged as evidence of how the legacy
86-task pack was consolidated into TASK-0022 to TASK-0045. Their `"status":
"ACTIVE"` fields and "active" wording describe that pack as it was written. They
do not say what is being worked on now, and the files are kept as they were
rather than rewritten.
