# Material texture credits

Every `<look>.png`, `<look>-normal.png` and palette file here is generated by
`tools/generate-material-textures.py`.

Most looks are original Arch Dock material (GPL-3.0-or-later), drawn from the
vector swatches (`<look>.svg`) with tileable noise.

The looks of real things are composed from real texture sets published by
ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com) under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
licence (public domain dedication), downloaded as `<Id>_1K-JPG.zip`:

| Files | ambientCG set |
| --- | --- |
| `magma.png`, `magma-normal.png` | Lava003 |
| `magma-ember.png` | Lava002 |
| `magma-blaze.png` | Lava005 |
| `magma-molten.png` | Lava004 |
| `river.png`, `river-deep.png`, `river-jade.png` (the river bed) | Gravel021 |
| `river-clear.png`, `river-sunset.png` (the river bed) | Gravel020 |

The water over the river beds, and `river-normal.png`, are computed by the
generator's `--real` stage: a wind-driven wave spectrum (Tessendorf,
"Simulating Ocean Water", 2001), the bed seen through water that absorbs light
with depth, the light the ripples focus on the bed, and the sky mirrored where
the ripples tilt.
